Play as Chiikawa, Hachiware, Usagi, Momonga or Doro in Terraria 1.4.4.9.
Riakawa changes the player appearance. Weapons, attacks, music, sounds and gameplay remain vanilla.
It also adds the Siren Island Jukebox (4 Wood at a Work Bench), which plays an original song when placed.
Choose a character or restore the original player in Mod Configuration. Korean and English supported.
Unofficial, noncommercial fan mod.
