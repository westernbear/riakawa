[h1]Riakawa: Chiikawa, Hachiware & Usagi[/h1]
치이카와·하치와레·우사기로 테라리아를 모험하세요. 캐릭터, 무기 외형, 공격 연출과 소리를 바꾸며 기본 게임의 전투 수치와 진행은 유지합니다.

Play Terraria as Chiikawa, Hachiware or Usagi. Character-specific skins for 426 vanilla weapons, attack effects, custom voices and emotes. Native combat stats, collision and progression are preserved.

[h2]In the mod / 모드 구성[/h2]
[list]
[*]Three animated characters; armor and body accessories hidden, wings and mounts retained.
[*]1,278 weapon variants covering inventory, dropped and held items, with projectiles, minions, whips, beams and chains.
[*]Press V to emote. Rebind in Controls. Emotes do not interrupt movement or combat.
[*]Character and emote synchronization in multiplayer.
[*]Korean / English settings: restore Original appearance, switch characters, toggle visuals, voices and effects.
[/list]

[h2]Install / 설치[/h2]
PC Steam Terraria 1.4.4.9 / tModLoader v2026.08.3.0.
Subscribe and enable Riakawa in tModLoader's Mods menu. Choose a character in Mod Configuration. Install the music pack separately and enable it in Use Resource Packs.
구독 후 모드 메뉴에서 활성화하세요. 캐릭터·효과음은 모드 설정에서, 감정표현 키는 조작 설정에서 변경합니다.
