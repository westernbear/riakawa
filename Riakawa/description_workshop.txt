[h1]Riakawa[/h1]
Play Terraria as Chiikawa, Hachiware, Usagi, Momonga or Doro.
치이카와·하치와레·우사기·모몽가·도로롱 중 한 캐릭터로 플레이할 수 있습니다.

[list]
[*]Animated player skins for movement, combat, swimming, mounts and damage.
[*]Doro runs on four legs at Hermes Boots sprint speed, swings swords with its own paw and aims guns at the cursor.
[*]Character choice syncs in multiplayer. Original appearance is always available.
[*]Weapons, attacks, music, sounds and game balance stay unchanged.
[*]Siren Island Jukebox: craft it from 4 Wood at a Work Bench and place it to play an original song. 나무 4개로 만드는 주크박스를 설치하면 새로 만든 곡이 흘러나옵니다.
[/list]

Requires PC Steam Terraria 1.4.4.9 and tModLoader v2026.08.3.0. Enable the mod and choose a character in Mod Configuration.
PC Steam 테라리아 1.4.4.9와 tModLoader v2026.08.3.0용입니다. 모드를 켠 뒤 모드 설정에서 캐릭터를 고르세요.
